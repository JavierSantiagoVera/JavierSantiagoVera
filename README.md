### Highlights

- 🎤 **2 peer-reviewed papers** — **SafeSurg @ MICCAI 2026 (oral)** and **CVAMD @ ICCV 2025**.
- 📈 **+7.5 mAP over the published baseline** on a 1,000-video, 54-institution surgical benchmark, without masks, boxes or text prompts.
- 🫀 **Multimodal model** fusing images and clinical records: **+11%** over image-only and **+50%** over tabular-only.
- 🧪 **2 years of hands-on deep learning research** — data pipelines, multi-GPU training, evaluation and error analysis, end to end.

### What I bring to a team

- **Model development:** CNNs, vision transformers (ViT, EVA-02) and foundation models, fine-tuned in PyTorch on multi-GPU servers.
- **Video & temporal modeling:** lightweight temporal heads over frame embeddings, designed and compared through ablation studies.
- **Multimodal learning:** fusing imaging with structured tabular data.
- **Rigorous evaluation:** fixed splits, multi-seed reporting, error analysis — results that hold up under peer review.
- **Real-world, messy data:** multi-institution medical data, limited annotations, class imbalance.
- **Communication:** 2 years as graduate TA in Visual Recognition, explaining CV/ML and debugging students' pipelines.
